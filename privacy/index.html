Which CodeShift privacy policy applies?

CodeShift Keyboard and CodeShift Terminal are separate apps, so each has its own privacy policy. This page helps you find the policy that applies to the app you use.

CodeShift Terminal does not yet have a separate Terms of Use. The CodeShift Keyboard terms apply only to the keyboard.

In short

  • CodeShift Keyboard has no internet permission, ads, analytics or user account. Learned words and pinned clipboard items stay on the device, and clipboard history is kept locally rather than sent to us.
  • CodeShift Terminal has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. It connects only to the servers you configure, and its saved hosts, credentials and SSH keys are encrypted on the device.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

The full privacy policies control if this summary and a policy ever differ.
